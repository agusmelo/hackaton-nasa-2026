See the infrared sky change

SPHEREx maps the whole sky every six months in 102 colours of infrared light. Compare its passes over the same patch and the things that moved or changed brightness stand out.

  1. Pick a targetType a name or coordinates, or choose an asteroid or comet. The examples above load in seconds.
  2. Blink the epochsPlay the passes as a short film. Difference mode turns anything that moved into a black-and-white pair.
  3. Measure what you findClick a source for its spectrum, its light curve and how fast it moves across the sky.

Detectors (wavelength)

SPHEREx's filter changes wavelength across each detector, so every exposure sees a target at a different wavelength. Comparing epochs within the same detectors keeps colour changes from looking like variability.
